Overview

What is this tool?

The SFUSD School Finder is a map-based tool designed to help families in San Francisco explore public schools near them. Instead of browsing a long list, you can see all 127 district schools plotted on an interactive map, filter by school level, and instantly get commute times from any home address.

Whether you are new to SFUSD, trying to understand how school assignments work, or simply curious about what options exist nearby, this guide walks you through the core features step by step.

Who this guide is for

This guide is written for parents and caregivers who are new to the SFUSD School Finder — you may be exploring for the first time, comparing options before an enrollment deadline, or helping someone else navigate the tool.

Features

What you can do

Here is a quick overview of the features available in the tool.

📍

Search from your address

Enter your home address to personalize commute estimates for every school on the map.

🗺️

Interactive map view

All 127 schools appear as numbered pins. Click any pin to see the school's name and quick details.

🔍

Search by school name

Already have a school in mind? Type its name to center the map on it and see nearby alternatives.

🏫

Filter by school level

Use the tabs to show only Elementary (65), Middle (24), or High (17) schools, or view all at once.

⏱️

Commute time radius

Toggle between 10, 20, and 30-minute radius views to narrow your map to schools within reach.

🚶

Multi-mode commute details

Each school shows walk, bike, drive, and Muni times so you can plan realistically.

📊

Full school profile

Tap "View full school profile" to access grades served, programs, and additional details.

⚖️

Side-by-side compare

Use the "Compare" button to evaluate two or more schools against each other.

📋

Nearby schools list

A ranked list of schools within your chosen radius appears beside the map, sorted by distance.

Getting started

Using the tool for the first time

Follow these steps to go from a blank map to a shortlist of schools in under five minutes.

1

Enter your home address

Type your street address in the "Home address in San Francisco" field and click Search. This personalizes all commute estimates to your actual location. You can also tap "Use current location" if browsing on a mobile device.

2

Choose a school level

Click Elementary, Middle, or High in the filter tabs at the top of the map. This reduces visual clutter and focuses the map on schools relevant to your child's grade. The number beside each label shows how many schools exist at that level.

3

Set a commute radius

Use the 10 / 20 / 30 min buttons above the map to draw a travel-time circle around your home. Schools within the circle are your most practical options. Start with 20 minutes — a common benchmark for many families.

4

Browse the map and the nearby list

Numbered pins appear on the map; a ranked list appears to the right, starting with the nearest school. The star icon marks your home; the red "H" marks a highlighted school. Click any pin or list item to open that school's commute panel.

5

Check commute details

The school panel on the right shows walk, bike, drive, and Muni times. These update to reflect your actual address once you have entered it. Use these numbers to think through your daily schedule.

6

Explore a school's full profile or compare options

Click "View full school profile" to see program details, grades served, and more. Or click "Compare" to put two schools side by side and evaluate them together.

7

Search for a specific school

If someone recommended a school, use the "Search by school name" field near the top. The map will center on that school and show nearby alternatives — useful for understanding whether a specific school fits into your commute.

Tip

Try searching with two different home addresses — your current home and a workplace or common drop-off point — to get a fuller picture of practical options.

Understanding what you see

A quick glossary of the terms and labels that appear in the tool.

Term or label What it means
Nearest school / Nearest Elementary The school closest to your entered address (or to the map center if no address is set). This updates when you change the level filter.
Numbered pins (1, 2, 3…) Each pin is one school, ranked by distance from your home. Clicking a pin opens its detail panel on the right side.
Star icon on the map Your home address, or the school you searched for by name.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to enter my address to use the tool?

No. The map loads with all schools visible and baseline commute times by default. Entering your address simply makes the commute estimates more accurate and personalizes the "nearest school" result.

What does "nearest school" mean — is that the school my child is assigned to?

No. "Nearest school" shows the SFUSD school geographically closest to your address. SFUSD uses a choice-based enrollment system, so proximity does not automatically mean assignment. This tool helps you explore options, not confirm placement.

Can I look at schools for different grade levels at the same time?

The filter tabs show one level at a time (Elementary, Middle, or High), or all schools together. If you need to compare across levels — for example, because you have children of different ages — switch between tabs and note the schools that interest you, then use the compare feature.

What does "Other" mean in the school level count?

"Other" covers SFUSD schools that do not fit neatly into the three main levels, such as early education centers (like Commodore Stockton Early Education School) that serve children younger than Kindergarten.

Are commute times real-time or estimates?

They are estimates based on typical conditions and are not real-time traffic data. Use them as a planning guide rather than precise measurements, especially for Muni and drive times that can vary with traffic.

What is the "Compare" button for?

Compare lets you place two schools side by side to evaluate them together — useful once you have narrowed your list to a few candidates and want to weigh commute, grades served, and other profile details against each other.

What's next

Where to go from here

Once you feel comfortable exploring the map, here are natural next steps to deepen your research.

📂

Read individual school profiles

Tap "View full school profile" on any school you like. Profiles include program specializations, language pathways, and other details that go beyond commute and location.

⚖️

Use the Compare feature

After building a shortlist of 2–4 schools, use Compare to see them side by side. This is especially useful before attending school tours or open houses.

📅

Check enrollment deadlines

The School Finder helps you understand your options, but enrollment itself happens through SFUSD's enrollment portal. Check the district website for current application windows and deadlines.

🏫

Attend school tours or information nights

Many SFUSD schools host open houses in fall and winter. Once you have a shortlist from the map, visiting in person gives you a feel for community and culture that no tool can replicate.
